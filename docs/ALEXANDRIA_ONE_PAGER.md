# Alexandria

## Content intelligence for websites that cannot afford to age

**Founder:** Jose, SEO agency owner  
**Public demo:** https://alexandria-content-intelligence--jose529.replit.app

> The first Alexandria burned. The modern one decays one outdated page at a time.

## The problem

Jose runs an SEO agency whose clients can have thousands of valuable web pages. Keeping every statistic, deadline, policy, requirement, and recommendation current is one of the agency's hardest recurring jobs.

- **Content at scale:** Manual audits are slow, expensive, and inevitably incomplete across thousands of pages.
- **Accuracy risk:** Outdated information erodes trust, weakens authority, and creates client escalations.
- **Perpetual change:** Even a freshly updated page begins aging again when new data, policies, or guidance appear.

This is not a one-time editing problem. It is a continuous intelligence problem.

## The solution

Alexandria watches the horizon for changes, finds which pages are affected, verifies the new information against authoritative sources, and prepares cited replacement copy for human approval.

1. A user enters a website.
2. You.com Search discovers priority pages and relevant recent sources.
3. You.com Contents reads the live page content.
4. You.com Research cross-checks claims and produces structured, cited findings.
5. Alexandria displays the current claim beside editor-ready replacement copy for review, copying, or download.

## Why it matters

Alexandria turns reactive content cleanup into a repeatable workflow. SEO teams, publishers, and agencies can focus human attention on the pages with the greatest accuracy and authority risk instead of rereading an entire content library.

## Built with

- **You.com Search API:** Discovery, freshness, and source coverage
- **You.com Contents API:** Clean live-page extraction
- **You.com Research API:** Multi-step reasoning, verification, citations, and structured output
- **Application:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Replit Autoscale

## Current MVP

- Public interactive product
- Evidence-backed Empowerly example
- Live priority-page scans with a valid You.com API key
- Cited findings with severity and confidence
- Before-and-after content patches
- Human approval plus copy/download actions
- Honest error states and no fabricated cross-domain demo findings
